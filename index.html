2020
Early 2020
Origin

Started programming

At 12, I began learning C# and C++, two programming languages used to build software, games, and performance-intensive applications. This was my introduction to writing code and understanding how software works.

I started with programming before I had any established business or trading career. Those early technical skills became the foundation for the projects I built later.

Late 2020
Underground

High-value Fortnite account sales

Later in 2020, I moved into the market for valuable Fortnite accounts. Accounts with rare cosmetics or older items could command high resale prices; some sold for more than $600 each.

I obtained accounts through scams. Although this generated money, it also marked my entry into an underground online economy built around stolen or improperly acquired digital accounts.

2021
Early 2021
Underground

Zaso Dev

In early 2021, I launched Zaso Dev, a program presented as a Fortnite “dev account” experience. In this context, that meant a modified version of the game running through a private server, where cosmetic items, including rare or unreleased-looking items, could appear unlocked without purchasing them.

The software also contained a remote-access trojan (RAT), malicious software that can let someone access another person’s computer without authorization. I used that access to steal cryptocurrency and online accounts. This was not a legitimate Fortnite developer account or an official Epic Games product.

2022
Early 2022
Underground

Account “pulling”

In early 2022, I became involved in what the community called “pulling” Fortnite accounts: obtaining control of an account by manipulating account-recovery or support processes rather than through the owner’s consent.

I impersonated legitimate account holders and using fabricated account information to deceive Epic Games employees. Accounts obtained this way were then resold, often for approximately $800 to $1,200. Social engineering is the use of deception to influence people into granting access or disclosing information.

Mid 2022
Underground

Discord development scene

By mid-2022, I had become active in Discord-based programming communities, developing DDoS-related tools and OSINT software. DDoS refers to distributed denial-of-service attacks, which overwhelm online services with traffic. OSINT means open-source intelligence: gathering and analyzing publicly accessible information.

I was also involved in more than 15 swatting incidents. Swatting is the dangerous practice of causing an emergency police response to be sent to someone through a false report; it can put innocent people and responders at serious risk. These activities were a major escalation beyond account trading.

Late 2022
New direction

Started day trading

In late 2022, I began learning day trading: opening and closing positions in financial markets over short periods, often within the same day, to try to profit from price movements.

This introduced a different way of using technical skills and market research to pursue income. It did not immediately replace my other activities, but it became an important part of my later work.

2023
Early 2023
Reputation

Known as “Best Puller”

By early 2023, I was known within that account-pulling community by the nickname “Best Puller,” reflecting the reputation I had developed for obtaining Fortnite accounts.

I had made more than $40,000 from those activities by that point. I had built a reputation and made serious money while I was still young, but the risks were growing too.

June 2023
Crypto incident

Access to a $5.6M wallet

In June 2023, I socially engineered someone into providing access to a cryptocurrency wallet that held approximately $5.6 million in tokens. A crypto wallet is a tool for controlling digital assets; having access to a wallet is not the same as lawfully owning its contents.

I did not move or receive those funds, and that the tokens were later drained by someone else. The $5.6 million was the wallet balance involved, not money I earned or kept.

Weeks later
Legal

Investigation and case dismissal

A few weeks after the wallet incident, I came under investigation for alleged cryptocurrency theft. Investigators seized my electronic devices, and I was facing a sentence of up to five years.

I was a minor at the time and was not jailed. After several months and approximately $15,000 spent on lawyers and related legal costs, the case was dropped due to lack of evidence.

2024
Early 2024
Trading

Reached trading profitability

In early 2024, I began to see positive trading results after spending time learning how markets move, practicing entries and exits, and dealing with losses.

This was the point when I became a profitable trader. What started as something I was learning in late 2022 had become a real source of income, and I kept building on it over the following years.

2025
Early 2025
Underground

Return and major losses

After staying quiet in the underground community for a period, I returned in early 2025. I used remote-access malware again and participating in a cryptocurrency rug pull involving more than $50,000. A rug pull generally describes a scheme in which people behind a crypto project or trading arrangement abruptly remove funds or liquidity, leaving others with losses.

During roughly the same period, I was also the victim of a separate rug pull totaling about $75,000. These were two different events. The $50,000 and $75,000 were separate events: one involving funds taken and the other money I lost.

Mid 2025
Turning point

Left the underground scene

By mid-2025, I decided that the risk of criminal charges, losing access to my devices, and potentially spending years in prison was no longer worth pursuing money through illegal online activity.

I describe this as my turning point: stepping away from account theft, malware, and related communities to focus on lawful software development, trading, and building businesses. It changed the direction I wanted my work and life to take.

Late 2025
Income

Five-figure year

By the end of 2025, I earned five figures during the year while still 16 to 17 years old. This followed my move toward legal income and continued involvement in trading.

“Five figures” means an amount from $10,000 to $99,999. I am keeping the exact amount private.

2026
Late 2026 · Now
Present day

Businesses, stocks, and crypto

Now, in late 2026 at age 18, I earned six figures this year while running multiple businesses and trading in the stock market. My work also includes software and online projects, alongside speculative cryptocurrency and memecoin trading.

I lost around $60,000 on memecoins in 2026 alone. Memecoins are highly speculative cryptocurrencies whose prices can move sharply with hype and market sentiment. My six-figure earnings and $60,000 memecoin losses tell two different parts of the same year.

My story, in my own words. 2020 to 2026. The $5.6 million wallet balance was never money I received.
